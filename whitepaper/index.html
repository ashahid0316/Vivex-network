Vivex Network introduces a paradigm shift: an energy-efficient, cloud-simulated token distribution consensus running on the Solana blockchain. By offloading heavy cryptographic ledger hashing to backend server infrastructure, smartphone users earn $VVX tokens without battery drain, CPU/GPU overheating, hardware wear, or mobile cellular data exhaustion.

  • Democratized Earning Velocity: Fair base rates for pioneers across 150+ countries.
  • Frictionless Onboarding: Google OAuth 2.0 single-click sign-in eliminating complex seed phrase barriers.

1.4 Demographic Expansion & Emerging Market Empowerment
